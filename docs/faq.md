# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Coupit?
Coupit is a platform that helps users manage their transactions and financial data in a secure and efficient manner.

### Is Coupit free to use?
Coupit offers both free and premium plans. The free plan includes basic features, while the premium plan offers advanced functionality.

### How do I get started?
1. Sign up for an account
2. Complete your profile
3. Connect your accounts
4. Start using the platform

## Technical Questions

### What browsers are supported?
Coupit supports the latest versions of:
- Chrome
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge

### Is there a mobile app?
Yes, Coupit is available as a mobile app for both iOS and Android devices.

### How secure is my data?
We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. For more details, see our [Security Guidelines](security.md).

## Account Management

### How do I reset my password?
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email
4. Follow the instructions in the email

### Can I change my email address?
Yes, you can change your email address in the account settings.

### How do I delete my account?
1. Go to account settings
2. Select "Delete Account"
3. Confirm your decision
4. Follow the verification steps

## Features

### What types of transactions can I track?
You can track:
- Income
- Expenses
- Transfers
- Investments
- Custom categories

### Can I export my data?
Yes, you can export your data in various formats including CSV and PDF.

### Is there an API available?
Yes, we provide a REST API for developers. See our [API Documentation](api.md) for details.

## Billing

### How do I upgrade my plan?
1. Go to billing settings
2. Select "Upgrade Plan"
3. Choose your new plan
4. Complete the payment

### Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your premium features will remain active until the end of your billing period.

### Do you offer refunds?
We offer refunds within 14 days of purchase if you're not satisfied with the service.
